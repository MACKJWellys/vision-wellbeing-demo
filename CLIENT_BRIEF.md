## Target Audience

**Who is the primary customer?**
Adults (25-45) experiencing anxiety, overthinking, perfectionism, low mood, or burnout — particularly high-functioning professionals who recognise something needs to change but haven't found the right fit. Secondary: parents seeking CBT for their young people.

**What problem does the customer have?**
Stuck in patterns of worry, overthinking, perfectionism, or low mood that self-help hasn't shifted. May have tried therapy before and found it vague or unhelpful. Want something structured, evidence-based, and practical — not just "talking about feelings." Nervous about starting therapy or sceptical it will work online.

**What does the customer want to feel when visiting this site?**
Understood. Not judged. Like they've found someone who explains their problem better than they can themselves. Reassured by qualifications and research, but warmed by the human, collaborative tone. Ready to take the step because the barrier to entry (free consultation) feels low.
